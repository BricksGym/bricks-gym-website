Not legal advice. These items need a California attorney's review before launch. The draft agreement is in `src/content/agreement.ts` (shown at `/legal/membership-terms`).

## Health Studio Services Contract Law (Civ. Code §1812.80 et seq.)

1. **Right to cancel.** The old paper form's 24-hour cancellation window has been replaced with a 5-business-day right to cancel (excluding Sundays and holidays), with a refund within 10 days. Confirm the exact statutory wording, type size (at least 10-point bold) and placement near the signature line.
2. **Existing members** signed agreements with the 24-hour clause. Ask whether they need notice or any correction.
3. **Death and disability; moving more than 25 miles.** Confirm that the $99 relocation fee and the $149 medical-cancellation fee are allowed, given the statute's cancellation rights for these events.
4. **Payment term vs. contract term.** Confirm the post-term month-to-month continuation complies. (Last month's dues are no longer collected up front.)
4a. **Crossed-out enrollment fee.** The 24-month plan shows the usual $49.99 enrollment fee crossed out next to $0, on the website and the printed sheet. Confirm $49.99 is a price actually charged, so the former-price comparison is accurate (California and FTC rules).
4b. **Rates note.** The website and printed sheet say "Only valid until Dec. 31, prices subject to change." Confirm price changes for existing members follow the 7–30 day notice in the agreement.
5. **Ways to cancel.** The owner wants cancellations handled in person. The agreement now accepts cancellation in person, by email from the address on file (the website links a pre-written cancellation email), and by first-class mail. The online cancellation form was removed. Confirm this is sufficient now that members sign up and sign online.
6. **Non-refundable fees** (Condition 8). Confirm the exceptions are worded correctly.

## Automatic Renewal Law (Bus. & Prof. Code §17600 et seq., incl. AB 2863)

7. **How sign-up works now:** members sign the agreement online, so this is an online sign-up under the ARL. The online way to cancel offered is a pre-written termination email (linked in the footer and FAQ). Confirm this satisfies Bus. & Prof. Code 17602(d) as amended by AB 2863.
8. **Auto-renewal consent:** the website collects a separate, unchecked acknowledgment. The binding consent must also be captured separately (unchecked) on the Club Systems agreement, and stored.
9. **Acknowledgment email, annual reminder, price-change notice (7–30 days):** promised in the agreement. Confirm Club Systems sends them, or set up a manual process.
10. **Cancellation requests by email:** the gym replies to confirm and processes it in Club Systems. Confirm no retention offer may delay the cancellation.

## Other agreement terms (kept as written, flagged for review)

11. **Release of liability**, including the club's negligence and illnesses such as COVID-19. California courts won't enforce a release that covers gross negligence. Review the wording.
12. **Early cancellation fees:** 6-month (placeholder $149), 12-month ($199 or remaining dues, whichever is less), 24-month ($299: confirm whether "or remaining dues, whichever is less" applies).
13. **Arbitration (disputes over $1,000, AAA, jury waiver).** The draft references AAA's Consumer Arbitration Rules. Review enforceability and the fees the business would owe.
14. **Collections** (3 months unpaid or a past-due balance over $100 / $150) and **default acceleration** (Condition 13).
15. **Minors:** members aged 14–17 can't be bound by contract. The form requires the Primary member to be 18 or older and the parent or guardian. Confirm the guardian signature and responsibility language.
16. **Driver's license numbers:** collected online and emailed to the gym. Under California data-breach law, these count as personal information. Review storage, the practice of deleting each email after entry, and breach-notice obligations.
17. **Privacy policy** (`/legal/privacy`): review, including whether the CCPA/CPRA applies.
18. **Liability waiver** (`/legal/waiver`) for day-pass visitors.
19. **Legal business name:** placeholder until the entity is final.

## Online sign-up and electronic signature (added 2026-11-25)

- **Electronic signature.** Members now sign the agreement online: they scroll through the full agreement, type their initials once and check a box for each section that needs initials, agree to automatic renewal with a separate checkbox, then type their full name and check an electronic-signature box. The gym records the date, time, IP address and agreement version, and a signed copy is emailed to the member right away. Confirm this meets California's e-signature law (UETA, Civil Code 1633.1 and following) and the health studio contract rules (Civil Code 1812.80 and following), including giving the buyer a copy.
- **Payment by phone.** The website never collects card or bank numbers. Staff call the member and type the card directly into Club Systems. Confirm the agreement's payment authorization covers a card given by phone after signing.
- **Parent or guardian.** For members under 18, the Primary member checks a box confirming they are the parent or guardian and signs for them. Confirm this is enough, or whether a separate guardian signature is needed.
